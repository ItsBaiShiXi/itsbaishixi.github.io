## What I'm building

**[We Snow](https://www.togetherwesnow.app/appDownload)** — a ski trip-planning app I
co-founded, now with several hundred registered users on iOS and Android. Pick a mountain, get a
group together, sort out carpools, and split the costs afterwards without anyone doing arithmetic
on a napkin.

Most of my work on it is the language-model side: filling gaps in the resort database with LLM
pipelines, building fuzzy semantic search over it with RAG, and turning live resort weather
telemetry into personalised travel and gear recommendations. The other half is less glamorous and
just as satisfying — an automated multi-party expense split, and a database schema redesign that
cut authentication latency.

## What I'm researching

I'm a **graduate research assistant in human-computer interaction** at UC Irvine, looking at how
people actually work with AI-assisted systems — which turns out to be a different question from
how good the system is on its own. Still in progress, so there isn't much to link yet.

Before that, robot learning: how much of a vision-language-action model's competence lives in the
backbone versus the action head, and what breaks when you shrink either one.

## Recent writing

**[Variational Autoencoders in Topic Modeling](/posts/avitm-topic-modeling/)** — reimplementing
AVITM in PyTorch and checking whether a learned inference network really does match classical
topic-model inference at a fraction of the cost. It does: an 88% cut in training time at
competitive perplexity and coherence.

**[How Much Policy Does a VLA Need?](/posts/vla-policy-size/)** — scaling only a VLA's diffusion
policy head to find out how much capacity the action policy really needs.

**[Human-Like Fox v Fox Bots for Melee](/posts/melee-fox-bots/)** — whether reinforcement learning
started from professional demonstrations beats the same pipeline started from amateur ones.
