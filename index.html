What I work on

Robot learning, and specifically the question of how much model you actually need. A lot of that is vision-language-action models: how much of a VLA's competence lives in the backbone versus the action head, and what you give up when you shrink either one. I care about the latency side of this as much as the benchmark side — a policy that scores well and acts sluggishly isn't much use on real hardware.

The thing I keep relearning is that the interesting result is usually the one that failed for a structural reason rather than a numerical one.

Recent work

How Much Policy Does a VLA Need? — a CS295 final report with Eric and Sean. Freezing a VLA's vision and language stacks and scaling only the diffusion policy head, to find out how much capacity the action policy really needs.
